Getting here

Venue & Travel

Linden House sits right on the Thames at Hammersmith — a beautiful Edwardian riverside venue. 60 Upper Mall, London W6 9TA.

⊙
By Tube
Ravenscourt Park (District line) is a 5 minute walk. Stamford Brook and Hammersmith are also nearby options.
✈
From Heathrow
Take the Piccadilly line direct to Hammersmith — approximately 40 minutes. From there it's a short walk or taxi along the river to the venue.
⊡
By Car
Limited parking on Upper Mall. We recommend arriving by public transport or taxi. Paid parking available on King Street nearby.
◎
By Taxi
Ask for Linden House, 60 Upper Mall, W6 9TA. The venue is tucked along the riverside path — your driver may need guidance on the final approach.
◇
Accommodation
We recommend nearby hotels in Hammersmith and Chiswick. See our accommodation guide for curated options at various price points.
